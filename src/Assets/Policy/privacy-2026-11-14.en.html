Mojitto Privacy Policy

Tapas (타파스) ("we", "us", or the "Company") sets out this Privacy Policy under the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea, to protect your personal information and to handle any concerns about it quickly.

This policy applies to the Mojitto app (Android and iOS) and to the website mojitto.bar. On the stores we appear as "Tapas Maker" (Google Play) and "Junwoo Lee" (App Store, our representative).

This policy takes effect on November 14, 2026. (Announced on November 6, 2026)

If there is any difference between this English version and the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

At a glance

  • Your diary stays on your phone. Unless you choose to show it to friends in the Lounge, your diary titles, entries and photos are not sent to our servers. The times you tapped emotions and your past records are not sent either.
  • If you use the Cocktail Lounge, some things go to our servers. The Lounge is a feature you use by signing in with Kakao, Google or Apple. The cocktail and emotions of the day you bring into the Lounge, plus any labels, story and photos you choose to show to friends you pick, go to our servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea. We delete them 24 hours after you first enter the Lounge with that drink. However, reported content is kept for up to 90 days so we can review the report. Your Cheers history with a friend (both cocktails and the dates) is kept until you are no longer friends. Your Lounge account (name and friend list) is kept until you delete it.
  • You can delete your Lounge account in the app at any time. If you can't use the app, you can ask us by email by following https://mojitto.bar/delete-account/.
  • Usage statistics and crash information go to Google Firebase. From app version 2.0, you can turn each of them off in More › App Information › Usage Stats · Crash Info (versions before 2.0 have no setting to turn them off). They never include your diary entries or photos, but the statistics do include summary values such as the type of cocktail you got that day and how many emotions you tapped. We don't show ads and we don't collect advertising IDs.
  • The Lounge is only for people aged 14 or older.

This summary is here to help you understand the policy. The full text below is what applies.

1. Why we process personal information

We process personal information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, we will tell you in advance and follow the steps the law requires.

  1. Diagnosing app errors and answering your questions
    To find the cause when the app has an error, and to answer your questions (for example about payments).
  2. Checking and restoring Gold (subscription and lifetime membership)
    To check that a payment is valid, and to restore Gold when you change phones or reinstall the app.
  3. Usage statistics
    To learn which features are used and how often, how stable the app is, and where Gold purchases get stuck, so we can fix and improve the service. Your diary entries and photos are never part of these statistics. We don't use this for ads or for other companies' purposes.
  4. Backup (only if you turn it on)
    To keep your records in your own cloud storage.
  5. Providing the Cocktail Lounge
    To create your Lounge account and confirm your sign-in; to connect you with friends; to deliver the day's drink, emotions, labels and story to the friends you choose; to detect and record Cheers; and to send Lounge notifications.
  6. Keeping the Lounge safe
    To handle reports and blocks, prevent misuse, and suspend Lounge use when necessary; and to fix server problems and keep our servers secure.
  7. Confirming you are 14 or older
    The Lounge is only for people aged 14 or older, so we confirm this.

We process only the minimum information needed for these purposes.

2. What personal information we process

A. Information stored on your phone

Diary titles, entries and attached photos; emotion records and the times you tapped them; cocktail results; labels; your nickname; reminder times (the hour is sent as usage statistics — Section 2-D); your screen-lock passcode; your biometric-unlock setting; unfinished stories (drafts); app settings.

  • This information stays in your phone's storage. Except for the cases described in B to F below, it is never sent to our servers.
  • Stories, photos and label names from days you did not bring into the Lounge, past records, reports and search terms are not sent to our servers. However, each time you SHAKE-IT, the values below are sent to Google Firebase as usage statistics (Section 2-D), on both Android and iOS (from app version 2.0, they are not sent if you turn off usage statistics).
    • the type of cocktail you got
    • your emotion count and share of positive emotions (as ranges, not exact numbers; which emotions you tapped is never sent)
    • whether labels are attached, the number of labels (that day's total and the most attached at once), and the range of how many labels you own
    • whether a photo is attached
    • whether the record is for today, or how many days back it is
    • the hour you shook it (hour only)
    • whether reminders are on, and the reminder hour (hour only)
    • (Android) whether you use Google Drive backup
  • When you unlock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint, your phone's system does the check. The app only learns whether it succeeded. It never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • Home-screen and lock-screen widgets only read records on your phone to draw their pictures. They don't connect to the internet.

B. Sent to our servers to diagnose errors and answer questions (Android)

Nickname, device identifier, error messages and where the error happened, app version, OS version, and the order number if the error is about a payment.

  • If restoring a backup is rejected, we send only the reason and the date. We don't send diary content. (If a record sent by an old version of the app contains diary content, our server removes it and keeps only the date before saving.)
  • The Android app also sends errors that happen while a reminder goes off or auto-backup runs, even when you haven't opened the app.
  • The device identifier is an irreversibly transformed version of your Android device's ID (ANDROID_ID). When you delete the app, the value saved on your phone is removed, but the same value is created again if you reinstall the app. It changes if you factory-reset your phone. This value may also be used for the crash information in D.
  • The iOS app does not send this information. However, the iOS app also sends the crash information in D (such as records of the app closing unexpectedly) to Google Firebase.

C. Sent to our servers to check Gold payments (Android)

Nickname, a purchase token used to confirm the payment, and the product type (subscription or lifetime membership).

  • The iOS app checks payments on your phone. It does not send payment information to our servers.

D. Usage statistics and crash information (collected automatically, Android and iOS — from app version 2.0 you can turn them off)

We use Google Firebase's analytics tool (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash-reporting tool (Firebase Crashlytics). What you can turn off from app version 2.0 is usage statistics and crash information. The basic sending for remote settings below can't be turned off (Section 7).

  • App usage: which features you use; the time of day you write records; whether reminders and backup are on, and the reminder hour (hour only); which cocktail you got; the ranges of your emotion count and share of positive emotions (never the types of emotions); the number of labels and the range of how many labels you own; whether a photo is attached; Lounge steps and their results (never names, text, label names or search terms); screens you visit (Android); backup and restore results (success or failure type, and ranges of duration, file size and record count); whether reminders went off and how late they were; and whether you received Lounge notifications. The Android app sends usage statistics even when you haven't opened the app. It sends the results when a reminder goes off, when auto-backup runs, and when a Lounge notification arrives. When a reminder goes off, it also sends the exact-alarm permission and battery-saving status at that moment. When the app starts without a screen, for example after your phone restarts, the app is updated or the time settings change, it records your app settings and the exact-alarm permission again. These values may also be sent when you haven't opened the app.
  • App settings: whether you are a Gold member, your cocktail color setting, whether auto-backup is on, whether you use labels, how often you SHAKE-IT (in levels only), garnishes you own and wear, and similar settings.
  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, time zone, an app installation ID (app instance ID); on Android, the exact-alarm permission and the app's battery-saving status (standby bucket and background restriction); and on iOS app versions before 2.0, the identifier for vendor (IDFV — the iOS app doesn't send it from version 2.0).
  • Approximate location: country and city estimated from your IP address. We don't collect precise location.
  • Crash information: device model, OS, memory and storage status, an installation ID, app activity just before the crash, and, in some Android reports, the device identifier in B. The crash-reporting tool also sends the following even when the app hasn't crashed:
    • a session record each time you open the app (start time, installation ID, app and device information — used to count the share of use without crashes; Android and iOS)
    • from iOS app version 2.0: when a backup or restore fails, a code for the type of failure (never diary content or file names)
  • Remote settings: When the app starts, it fetches feature on/off values and the minimum supported version from Google Firebase. When it does, your installation ID and its authentication token, app information (app version, build number and package name), and device information (OS version, language, the country set on your device, and time zone) are sent. Because the values are fetched over the internet, your IP address is also sent to Google. Sending the installation ID and its authentication token, app and device information, and IP address is needed for the app to work and can't be turned off (Section 7). While usage statistics are being sent (that is, if you haven't turned off usage statistics), the time you first opened the app and the "App settings" above (such as whether you are a Gold member) are also sent.

We don't show ads and we don't collect advertising IDs. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

E. If you turn on backup

  • Android — Google Drive backup: A backup file containing your full diary and attached photos is saved to an app-only area of your own Google Drive. Only the Mojitto app can use that area, and we can't see it either. The Google account address you connect is stored only on your phone.
  • iOS — iCloud auto-backup: Off by default. If you turn it on, a backup file containing your full diary and photos is kept in an app-only area of your own iCloud Drive, and only the most recent one is kept. It doesn't appear in the Files app, and we can't see it either.
  • Backup to a file: You save a backup file to a place you choose (another app, Files and so on). It doesn't go through our servers.
  • Your iPhone's iCloud device backup may also include your Mojitto diary. Apple handles that backup; the app does not send it.

F. If you use the Cocktail Lounge (signed-in users only)

The Lounge is only available after you sign in. If you don't sign in, the Lounge sends nothing to our servers. Lounge information is stored on servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea (for sign-in records and notifications, see Section 7).

1) Lounge account

  • The identifier of your sign-in service: your Kakao member number, Google user ID or Apple user ID, whichever you used (see H below)
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's email address, name and profile photo URL (stored in Google Firebase's sign-in records; not copied to the Lounge database)
  • Your internal Lounge ID, your Lounge name (the same as your app nickname), and your invite code
  • When you signed up, and the last day you used the app (recorded once a day when you open the app while signed in)
  • When you confirmed you are 14 or older (we don't ask for your date of birth)
  • When you agreed to the Terms of Use and confirmed you had read this Privacy Policy, and which version (for this version, 2026-11-14)
  • Your Lounge notification settings

If you change your app nickname while signed in, your Lounge name changes too, including the copies your friends have. (Names already saved in block lists and reports stay as they were at that time.)

2) The drink you bring into the Lounge for the day

  • The day's cocktail, the time you shook it, the diary date, the glass shape and color, and the garnish you're wearing
  • The types and counts of the day's emotions
  • The sharing range you chose for each friend
  • We never send the times you tapped emotions.

When you enter the Lounge, the types and counts of the day's emotions are stored on our servers. A friend sees them only after you Cheers with that friend, and only if you chose to show "Emotions" or more to that friend.

3) Labels, story and photos — only if you choose a friend to see them

  • Label names and counts, and the emotions you attached the labels to: only if you choose at least one friend to see "Labels" or more
  • Story title, text and photos: only if you choose at least one friend to see "Story"
  • Only the record for that day, written on the phone you entered the Lounge with, is sent. If no friend is left to see it, we delete it right away.
  • Photos: before saving, our server makes a new copy (shrinking it if its long side is over 1280 pixels) and saves only a copy with location and other embedded data (EXIF) removed. We don't keep the original.

4) Friends, Cheers and blocks

  • Friend list: your friend's Lounge name, the date you became friends, the sharing range you chose for that friend, and your Cheers count
  • Friend requests: the sender's Lounge name, when it was sent, and the garnish the sender was wearing
  • Cheers history: when two drinks first clinked, both people's cocktails and diary dates, and the glass shapes and colors. It contains no emotions. Note that the cocktail itself is chosen from that day's emotions.
  • Cheers check record: when the Cheers happened and when each drink was shaken (used only to check Cheers; deleted 24 hours after the last Cheers)
  • Block list: the blocked person's Lounge name (at the time of blocking), when you blocked them, whether they were a friend, whether you blocked them through a report, and the sharing range you had chosen for them just before

5) Reports and suspensions

  • Report record: the internal Lounge IDs of the reporter and the reported person, the reported person's name at the time, the reason (chosen from a fixed list — there is no free-text box), and when it was reported
  • Report evidence: a copy of what the reporter could see of the other person at the moment of the report, based on the sharing range and Cheers (label names, the full story title and text, and photos), plus the cocktail and diary date. Emotions are not included in report evidence.
  • Suspension record: the number of reports, whether Lounge use is suspended and when, and an operator's note

6) Notifications

  • Your phone's push notification token, phone type (Android or iOS), and when it was registered — up to 10 phones per person
  • Notification log: who sent which kind of notification to whom and when (to limit how many notifications are sent per day)

The push token is created and registered only after you sign in to the Lounge.

7) Preventing misuse

  • The number of requests per feature, per day (to block excessive requests)

G. Server access logs (created automatically)

When the app communicates with our servers (the Lounge, and the servers in B and C), the following is recorded automatically:

IP address, app and device information (user agent — on iOS this includes the app build number and OS version), time of the request, the name of the function called, app type and version (in error-diagnosis logs), and your internal Lounge ID (for the Lounge).

  • Lounge server logs don't record names, emotions, text, push tokens or Kakao member numbers. Error-diagnosis and payment-check logs (B and C above) include your nickname and are deleted after 30 days. Lounge server access logs are kept for 3 months as required by law, then deleted (Section 3).

How to refuse a transfer, and what happens if you do

  • Lounge: If you don't sign in to the Lounge, or you delete your Lounge account, the Lounge items above are no longer transferred. In that case you can't use the Lounge. You can keep using your diary.
  • If you only don't want Lounge notifications, you can turn them off in the app's Lounge notification settings.
  • Statistics and crash information: From app version 2.0, you can turn off usage statistics and crash information separately in More › App Information › Usage Stats · Crash Info. When you turn off usage statistics, they are no longer transferred from then on. The app then deletes this phone's statistics ID and user properties and replaces the ID with a new one, so it is no longer linked to information already sent. When you turn off crash information in the same place, it is no longer transferred, starting the next time you open the app at the latest. However, crash reports and session records that were already queued for sending before you turned it off (because the phone was offline) may still be sent once the phone is back online. Either way, information already sent isn't deleted right away; it is deleted when the periods in Section 3 end. You can still use every feature of the app whichever you turn off. However, if you turn off crash information, we won't know when an error happens on your phone, so it may be harder to find and fix. All versions before 2.0 have no setting in the app to turn this off. On those versions, if you don't want this, update to 2.0 once it is out and turn it off, or delete the app, and nothing more will be transferred. Your diary is stored only on your phone, so please back it up before deleting the app.
  • Remote settings: Of the remote settings fetched when the app starts (Section 2-D), sending the installation ID, app and device information, and IP address is needed for the app to work and can't be turned off. (The time you first opened the app and your app settings are left out when you turn off usage statistics.)
  • Error-diagnosis records (Section 2-B — remote logs sent to our servers): These are needed for the app to work and can't be turned off (Android).
  • Payments: If you don't buy Gold, no payment information is transferred.
  • Support email: If you don't email us, nothing is transferred. You can report, block and delete your account in the app, and you can also make objections, access requests or account-deletion requests (when you can't use the app) by phone as in Section 16.

8. Children under 14

  • We don't knowingly process Lounge information of children under 14. If we learn that a child under 14 has created a Lounge account (including when a legal guardian tells us), we delete that account.
  • Features other than the Lounge, such as the diary, don't ask your age. They process only the information in Section 2-A to 2-E and the server access logs (Section 2-G) created when B or C is sent. They never process the Lounge information in Sections 2-F and 2-H. 9. Sensitive content that may be shown, and how to keep it private

  1. We don't ask for or collect sensitive information, such as information about your health. However, Mojitto is an app for recording emotions, so if label names or the text and photos of a story include sensitive matters such as your health, friends you chose "Labels" or more (label names) or "Story" (text and photos) for may see them.
  2. How to keep it private
    • You can choose a sharing range for each friend when you enter the Lounge and at any time after that (Hidden, Emotions, Labels, Story). Lowering it or choosing Hidden takes it down from that friend right away.
    • If no friend is set to "Labels" or more, label names are not sent to our servers; if no friend is set to "Story", your story is not sent.
    • Leaving the Lounge deletes the day's drink (emotions, labels, story and photos) right away.

10. Automated decisions — automatic Lounge suspension

  • If 5 or more different users report the same account, that account's Lounge use is suspended automatically, without a person reviewing it. This keeps accounts with many reports from reaching other users.
  • This decision uses only the number of reports (the number of different reporters). It does not use the reported content (labels, stories and so on).
  • When an account is suspended, its drinks in the Lounge, copies sent to friends, and pending friend requests it sent are removed. The diary and records on the phone are not touched.
  • A suspended user can ask for an explanation or object by emailing mojitto.bar@gmail.com (they can also call the number in Section 16). They can send their own comments with it. A person will review the case again and tell you the result within 30 days of receiving the request.
  • Our operator can also suspend or lift a suspension after reviewing reports.

Delete it in the app

You are signed out right away, and your drink disappears from your friends' screens. If you signed in with Apple, an Apple confirmation appears at the end. Closing it cancels the deletion.

Request it without the app
If you lost your phone, removed the app, or can't sign in, you can email mojitto.bar@gmail.com by following https://mojitto.bar/delete-account/ (you can also call the number in Section 16). We will confirm it's you, delete the account and reply.

What is deleted, and when

  • Right away: sign-in information (Google Firebase sign-in records and your signed-in state), your invite code, the record used to find your account by Kakao member number, the address for your notifications (push tokens), and drinks you brought into the Lounge together with the labels, stories and photos you shared with them (they also disappear from friends' screens)
  • Starting a few minutes later, step by step, within 24 hours: your Lounge name, friendships, Cheers history, friend requests you sent and received, your block list, sharing range settings, reports you made and their evidence, and suspension records
  • Deletion can't be undone. If you sign in again the same way, a new empty account is created.

What remains after deletion

  • Reports other people made about you, and their evidence: up to 90 days from each report. Your name is removed. This is to handle reports and prevent abuse.
  • Blocks other people made against you: they remain in that person's block list as an internal number only, without your name, and disappear when that person unblocks you or deletes their account.
  • Cheers check records on your former friends' side, notification logs and request-rate records: deleted automatically within two days at most.
  • Lounge server access logs: 3 months (kept as required by the Protection of Communications Secrets Act; they may remain a few days longer because of how the cleanup job runs) · other server logs: 30 days
  • If you asked by email: that email, for 30 days after our reply
  • Google's backups of Google Firebase sign-in records: up to 180 days

Sign-in connections

  • If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to disconnect Sign in with Apple when you delete your account.
  • If you signed in with Kakao, the app also asks Kakao to disconnect when you delete your account.
  • The app does not disconnect Google. You can do it yourself in Google Account › Security › Your connections to third-party apps & services.
  • If disconnecting fails, you can also do it yourself (Kakao: KakaoTalk Settings › Kakao Account › Account Use › External Service · Apple: iPhone Settings › Apple Account (your name) › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple).

Gold and your diary
Gold memberships and subscriptions are separate from your Lounge account, so deleting your account neither deletes nor cancels them. To cancel a subscription, use subscription management in Google Play or the App Store. The diary on your phone stays as it is, whatever happens to your Lounge account.

13. How we destroy personal information

  1. Information stored on your phone is deleted from the phone when you delete the app. We are not involved in this.
  2. Information on our servers is deleted without delay once its retention period in Section 3 ends or its purpose is fulfilled.
    • A cleanup job that runs every 10 minutes deletes Lounge information whose retention period has ended. An expiry setting (TTL) and storage lifecycle rules delete it again as a safety net.
    • Photos are checked and deleted three ways: deleted on the spot, by the cleanup job, and by storage lifecycle rules.
    • Support emails are deleted from the mailbox and its trash within 5 days after the period in Section 3 ends.
  3. Electronic files are deleted so that they can't be recovered. Our Lounge photo storage does not keep deleted files (soft delete is off).
  4. We don't make separate backups of the Lounge servers. However, because of how the database works, deleted information may remain in the system in its earlier state for up to 1 hour.
  5. Deletion when you delete your account follows Section 12.
  6. Information that must be kept by law is stored separately from other information and deleted when that period ends. The information we currently keep by law, with its legal basis and period, is listed at the end of Section 3.

14. How we keep personal information safe

  1. Only what's needed goes to our servers. Your diary stays on your phone. Only the one drink you bring into the Lounge for the day is sent, and labels, stories and photos are sent only if you choose friends to see them. We never send the times you tapped emotions. We never put the types of emotions into Lounge server logs, Lounge notifications or usage statistics.
  2. We keep it briefly. Lounge drinks and their content are deleted 24 hours after you first enter. Reported content and Cheers history are kept for the periods in Section 3.
  3. We remove photo location data. Our server re-creates each photo and saves it without location or other embedded data, and doesn't keep the original. Photos are delivered without public links, only after checking every time that the viewer is allowed to see them.
  4. You can read only your own data. The database lets each user read only their own information, and only our server can write to it. Report and suspension records can't be read by the app.
  5. Friends' content is not kept on your phone. Stories and photos received from friends are held only in memory while shown on screen, and are not saved to the phone's storage or cache. On Android, screenshots are blocked on the full-screen photo view.
  6. Encrypted connections. All information between the app and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  7. Minimal logs. Lounge server logs don't record names, emotions, text, push tokens or Kakao member numbers. Error-diagnosis and payment-check logs (Section 2-B and 2-C) include your nickname and are deleted after 30 days. We don't store sign-in service tokens on our servers.
  8. Limited viewing. We don't view stories and photos posted in the Lounge for operating the service. We check only reported content, and only when needed to handle a report.
  9. Access control. Access to systems that process personal information is given only to the minimum number of people who need it for their work.
  10. We follow a written plan. We have an internal management plan for protecting personal information and check at least once a year that we follow it. Computers and phones used to handle personal information are fully encrypted and kept up to date with security updates.

15. Automatic collection tools, and how to refuse them

  1. The Mojitto app doesn't use cookies. We don't show ads and we don't collect advertising IDs. (The Android app has advertising ID collection turned off in our analytics tool, and the iOS app uses an analytics tool that doesn't use advertising IDs.) The website mojitto.bar doesn't use cookies or analytics tools either, and we don't separately collect records of visits to the website. However, the website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub), and its fonts are loaded from jsDelivr. So when you open the website, connection information your browser sends, such as your IP address, is passed to these two services. Each service processes this information for security or statistics under its own privacy policy, and we don't receive or see it.
  2. We include Google Firebase tools in the app for usage statistics and crash reporting. What they collect is listed in Section 2-D.
  3. When the app communicates with our servers, server access logs (Section 2-G) are created automatically. They are essential to run the servers and can't be refused. Even if you don't use the Lounge, the Android app creates these logs when it sends error-diagnosis records or checks a Gold payment.
  4. If you don't want usage statistics collected: see Section 11-5.
  5. We don't use your behavioral information for personalized ads, and we don't let other companies collect it for personalized ads. (We have turned off the Google signals feature of our analytics tool and all data-sharing settings that would let Google use the statistics for its own products or for industry benchmarks.)
